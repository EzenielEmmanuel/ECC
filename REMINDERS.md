A living capture list for ideas, reminders, and future work across ORIS and the ECC resource registry. Add new entries whenever inspiration strikes. Checking a box means the work has been completed and verified, not merely discussed.

## How to use

- Add a new unchecked `- [ ]` item for each new idea.
- Add subtasks under larger ideas.
- Move an item to completed only after verification.
- Keep this list separate from live automated reminders: entries here do not send scheduled alerts on their own.
